AI System Prompts

The Prompts We Used — And Why

Three specialized AI prompts power the system. Each was designed with specific reasoning about when to act, what channel to use, and when to do nothing.

Classification Prompt
backend/app/services/classifier.py — CLASSIFICATION_PROMPT
Analyzes every failed payment, abandoned cart, and overdue invoice to determine the best recovery strategy. Returns structured JSON with category, probability, channel, timing, and reasoning.
Why this design: The prompt teaches the AI to think like a triage nurse — not every failure deserves the same response. A UPI timeout at 9 PM is network congestion (high recovery), not user abandonment. A stolen card gets zero outreach. The AI sees error_description, amount, method, and context to make nuanced decisions that hardcoded rules cannot.
You are the classification engine for a payment recovery system used by Indian merchants on Razorpay. Your job: analyze a failed payment, abandoned cart, or overdue invoice and determine the best recovery strategy. ## How to think about this 1. READ the error_description carefully - "Payment timed out" with method=upi means network congestion, NOT user abandonment - "Card declined" is ambiguous — look at amount and error_code for clues - "Bank server unavailable" is temporary — very high recovery if you wait 2. CONSIDER the amount — it affects urgency and channel choice - High amounts (>₹5000) justify WhatsApp over email - Low amounts (<₹200) on cart abandonment are likely low-intent browsing 3. PICK the right channel for the situation - WhatsApp: urgent, time-sensitive, high-recovery scenarios - Email: formal, non-urgent, detailed - SMS: brief reminder, secondary nudge - None: fraud, stolen cards, permanently blocked accounts 4. DETECT user-initiated cancellations - Category should be "user_cancelled" — NOT a gateway error - Don't send an immediate retry — wait at least 1 hour 5. SET timing based on what will actually help 6. ESTIMATE recovery_probability honestly based on ALL signals Categories: upi_timeout, bank_downtime, card_expired, insufficient_funds, gateway_error, user_cancelled, unrecoverable_decline, high_intent_abandonment, browse_only_abandonment, recently_overdue, moderately_overdue, long_overdue
Escalation Decision Prompt
backend/app/services/escalation.py — ESCALATION_PROMPT
After the first recovery attempt, the escalation engine runs every 5 minutes. The AI analyzes full attempt history — which channels were tried, what succeeded/failed, what's blocked — and decides the best next move.
Why this design: No hardcoded channel rotation. The AI sees what actually happened: "WhatsApp was sent but customer didn't act → try email with more detail." It respects the dynamic attempt budget (max_attempts computed per event) and has three layers of give-up prevention so the system never abandons a customer prematurely.
You are the escalation decision agent for a payment recovery system. ## Decision framework 1. LOOK at previous attempts AND blocked/failed channels: - If WhatsApp sent but no action → try email with more detail or SMS - If delivery failed → SWITCH to a different channel - Check "blocked_channels" — these hit a cooldown. ALWAYS pick different. - If a "backup_plan" is provided, follow it when primary didn't work. 2. CONSIDER the event context: - High amount + multiple attempts → customer is hesitating, not forgetting - UPI timeout → high recovery chance, keep trying different channels 3. ADJUST tone: - After 0-1 attempts: friendly, helpful - After 2 attempts: direct, acknowledge follow-up - After 3+ attempts: honest, final chance 4. WHEN TO GIVE UP: - ONLY if attempt_count >= max_attempts - OR if ALL previous delivery attempts failed (bad contact info) - NEVER give up on the first escalation
Message Personalization Prompt
backend/app/services/message_generator.py — MESSAGE_PROMPT
Generates context-aware recovery messages for WhatsApp, SMS, and Email. Each message is personalized based on the failure reason, amount, customer name, and attempt number.
Why this design: "Like a helpful shopkeeper who noticed something went wrong" — not pushy, not corporate. Channel constraints are strict: WhatsApp max 200 chars with 1-2 relevant emojis, SMS max 140 chars with zero fluff, Email with specific subject + body structure. Tone escalates naturally across attempts: friendly → gentle reminder → direct → final honest message.
You write recovery messages for a payment recovery system used by Indian merchants. Your job: write a SHORT, WARM, HELPFUL message that makes the customer want to complete their payment. Not pushy. Not corporate. ## Channel constraints WhatsApp (max 200 chars): Conversational, warm, 1-2 relevant emojis SMS (max 140 chars): Ultra-brief, action-focused, no emojis Email: Subject max 60 chars + 2-3 short paragraphs. No "We apologize for the inconvenience." ## Tone by attempt - attempt 0: Helpful, no pressure. "Hey, looks like something went wrong." - attempt 1: Gentle reminder. "Just following up." - attempt 2: Slightly more direct. "Your payment is still pending." - attempt 3+: Final, honest. "Last reminder before we close this." IMPORTANT: Use {link} as placeholder for the payment URL. Do NOT invent a URL.
3-Model Fallback Chain
backend/app/services/ai_client.py — MODEL_CHAIN
All three prompts route through a single AI client with automatic model fallback via OpenRouter. If the primary model is down, the next one takes over seamlessly.
Why this design: In financial software, the system must never block waiting for an AI provider. The chain is ordered by speed + cost: Claude Haiku 4.5 (fastest, best reasoning) → Gemini 3.7 Flash (fast alternative) → GPT-4o-mini (cheapest fallback). If all three fail, rule-based classification keeps the pipeline running with 0% AI dependency.
MODEL_CHAIN = [ { "id": "anthropic/claude-haiku-4.5", "name": "Claude Haiku 4.5", "timeout": 12 }, { "id": "google/gemini-3.7-flash", "name": "Gemini 3.7 Flash", "timeout": 10 }, { "id": "openai/gpt-4o-mini", "name": "GPT-4o-mini", "timeout": 12 }, ] # Each model gets 1 retry with 0.5s backoff # Rate limits (429) → skip to next model immediately # Invalid JSON → retry once, then skip # All models exhausted → return None → rule-based fallback kicks in

What Broke

War Stories — The Bugs That Shaped the System

They asked for honesty. Here's every major bug, its root cause, and the lesson learned.

Bug #1
The Premature Give-Up
What: Events exhausted after 1 attempt with a budget of 5.
Cause: AI escalation schema defaulted action to "give_up" instead of "send". Partial AI JSON inherited the destructive default.
Fix: Changed default to "send". Added AI override + hard guard. Three layers of defense.
In financial software, a single safety check is never enough. Defense in depth.
Bug #2
The TOCTOU Race Condition
What: After fixing Bug #1, events #16 and #17 were still prematurely exhausted.
Cause: Two Celery tasks processing the same event concurrently. Task A reads "pending", Task B reads "pending", both proceed.
Fix: Conditional DB updates (.eq("status", "pending")) + per-event Redis locks + atomic exhaustion check.
Async systems need explicit serialization. "It works locally" means nothing.
Bug #3 ⭐ Star Story
The Ghost Writer Mystery
What: Event #18 marked exhausted with skip_reason=null and next_action_at still set. Impossible state — our code always sets both.
Cause: n8n workflows were still active, writing directly to Supabase, bypassing the FastAPI backend entirely. Two independent systems racing with no mutual awareness.
Fix: Unpublished all n8n workflows. Added PostgreSQL trigger as database-level defense that blocks premature exhaustion from ANY writer.
When you can't find the bug in your code, look for systems you forgot were still running.
Bug #4
WhatsApp Personalization
What: WhatsApp messages sending generic Twilio templates instead of AI-personalized text.
Cause: Twilio WhatsApp requires pre-approved content_sid templates — can't send custom text.
Fix: Prioritized Green API (free-form text) over Twilio WhatsApp. Twilio becomes template fallback.
Provider limitations shape architecture. Not all APIs are equal.
Bug #5
Dynamic Budget Discovery
What: Every event showing 1/5 attempts. No event exceeding one attempt.
Cause: Fixed max_attempts = 5 for every event. A $50 browse-only cart got the same budget as a $30,000 failed invoice.
Fix: Dynamic max_attempts computed from amount, probability, and failure category. Range: 0 to 5.
One-size-fits-all is the enemy of ROI. Every attempt has a cost.
Bug #6
Railway Build Failures
What: Railway wouldn't build the Python backend. Three attempts failed.
Cause: Manual Nix package configuration conflicted with Nixpacks auto-detection.
Fix: Removed all manual Nix config. Let Nixpacks auto-detect from requirements.txt.
Don't fight the platform. Auto-detection was designed for this.

FAQ

Anticipated Questions

How is this different from Razorpay's existing Failed Payment Recovery?

Razorpay's existing product focuses on subscription payment recovery via WhatsApp reminders. Recovery Router handles all three revenue leaks (payment failures, cart abandonment, overdue invoices) through a unified AI pipeline with dynamic attempt budgets, multi-channel fallback, and honest metrics that distinguish organic from AI-driven recovery.

Why not use Razorpay Agent Studio to build this?

Agent Studio provides building blocks. Recovery Router is the complete pipeline — classify, route, act, measure — with production-grade reliability: distributed locks, race condition handling, crash recovery, and database-level safety triggers.

Why three AI models instead of one?

Reliability. If Claude Haiku is down, Gemini takes over. If both are down, GPT-4o-mini. If all AI fails, rule-based classification keeps the system running. In financial software, the system must never block waiting for an AI provider.

How do you handle customer consent and spam?

Per-resource cooldowns (5 min per phone/email) prevent over-messaging. Quiet hours (9 PM – 9 AM IST). Dynamic budgets limit total outreach. Browse-only carts get zero attempts. User-cancelled events get max 2 gentle attempts.

What about the metrics? Are they real?

Ghost recovery prevention ensures organic payments (no outreach sent) are tracked as organic_recovery, never attributed to the system. Reconciliation uses 4-strategy matching with currency + amount validation. All results are from Razorpay test-mode simulated data — not production.

What would you build next?

Hinglish voice recovery (regional language support), merchant-specific model fine-tuning, A/B testing framework for recovery strategies, and integration with Razorpay's Magic Checkout for pre-emptive failure prevention.
